**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Requesting Account Deletion

Users of DermaScan may request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion, please send an email to:

**Email:** [UMN_MCP_A@gmail.com](mailto:UMN_MCP_A@gmail.com)

**Subject:** Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your DermaScan account.

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon successful verification of your request, we will delete:

* User account information
* User profile information
* Saved preferences
* Scan history
* Uploaded skin analysis images
* Associated application data stored in our systems

---

## Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

* Legal obligations
* Security and fraud prevention
* Technical troubleshooting
* Backup and recovery processes

Any retained information will be deleted once the retention period expires or when no longer required.

---

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days of verification.
